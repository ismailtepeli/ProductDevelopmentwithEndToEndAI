# Discovery question bank — Website + SEO

**R** = required before analysis. **O** = optional (ask, but "not sure yet" is fine).
Ask in this order; skip what an earlier answer already settled. Each question is given in Turkish and English — use the operator's `prompt_language`.

The form in `templates/client-questionnaire.html` asks the same questions; keep the IDs in sync if you edit either.

## A. Company and contact / Firma ve iletişim

| ID | R/O | TR | EN |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | R | Firmanızın adı ve ne iş yaptığınızı bir iki cümleyle anlatır mısınız? | What is your company's name, and what do you do, in a sentence or two? |
| A2 | R | Bu projede karar verici kim? Onaylar kimden gelecek? | Who is the decision maker on this project? Who gives approvals? |
| A3 | R | Size en hızlı nasıl ulaşırız (telefon, e-posta, WhatsApp)? Hangi saatlerde? | What is the fastest way to reach you (phone, email, WhatsApp), and when? |
| A4 | O | Rakipleriniz kimler? Sizi onlardan ayıran ne? | Who are your competitors, and what sets you apart from them? |

## Follow-up triggers (ask the operator, not only the client)

- Budget (J1) below the low estimate → ask whether to offer a phased MVP.
- Fixed date (J2) closer than the expected timeline → ask what can move: scope, date or price (rush multiplier).
- Online payments, member login or personal data → add Security specialist and DBA; ask about KVKK/GDPR responsibility.
- Copy written by the client (D2) → ask the operator for a content deadline; late content is the most common delay.
- No domain/hosting access (H1/H2) → flag as a launch risk now.
- Who owns the code and accounts after delivery → contract question for the operator.
